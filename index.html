BAOTT-Bachelor of Anaesthesia & Operation Theater Technology

B.Sc. Anaesthesia & Operation Theatre Technology is a healthcare-focused undergraduate program that trains students in anaesthesia assistance, operation theatre management, surgical procedures, and patient care. Students learn to prepare and maintain operation theatres, assist anaesthesiologists and surgical teams, handle medical equipment, and follow safety and infection-control procedures. Graduates can pursue career opportunities in hospitals, operation theatres, surgical centres, emergency departments, and healthcare institutions.
