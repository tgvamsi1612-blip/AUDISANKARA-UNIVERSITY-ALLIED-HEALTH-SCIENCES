Undergraduate Program

Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT)

School of Sciences and Allied Health Sciences

Duration

5 Years

(4 Years Academic + 1 Year Mandatory Clinical Internship)

Eligibility

Intermediate (BiPC)

With a minimum of 50% marks

Course Objective

The Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) program equips students with the knowledge and clinical skills to assess, diagnose, treat, and prevent physical disabilities and movement disorders. The course focuses on rehabilitation, pain management, exercise therapy, and promoting overall physical well-being through evidence-based physiotherapy practices.

Job Opportunities

BPT graduates can build rewarding careers in various clinical and healthcare specializations:
