Course Objective

The Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) program equips students with the knowledge and clinical skills to assess, diagnose, treat, and prevent physical disabilities and movement disorders. The course focuses on rehabilitation, pain management, exercise therapy, and promoting overall physical well-being through evidence-based physiotherapy practices.
