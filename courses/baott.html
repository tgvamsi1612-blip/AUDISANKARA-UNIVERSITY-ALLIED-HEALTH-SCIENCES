Bachelor of Anaesthesia & Operation Theatre Technology (B.AOTT)

School of Sciences and Allied Health Sciences

Duration

4 Years

(3 Years Academic + 1 Year Mandatory Clinical Internship)

Eligibility

Intermediate (BiPC)

With a minimum of 50% marks

Course Objective

The Bachelor of Anaesthesia & Operation Theatre Technology (B.AOTT) program prepares students to assist anaesthesiologists and surgical teams in delivering safe and efficient patient care. The course provides comprehensive training in anaesthesia technology, operation theatre management, surgical procedures, critical care, infection control, and emergency patient support.

Career Path

Operation Theatre Services Anaesthesia Technology Critical Care Emergency & Trauma Care Hospital Administration Academics
